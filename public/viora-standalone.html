Core Product Principles
• Single Priority Focus: 1 curated goal for 7 days rather than 30 milestones.
• Zero-Prep Kitchen Routines: 5 mins embedded during milk or snack time using steel bowls and sofa cushions.
• 1-Tap Nanny Delegation: Pre-drafted Hindi & English WhatsApp cues so nannies execute autonomously.
